Obviously not but if we pull it off together, it's more than 100 times the invest with only the small risk of a small marketing budget for you now.

## Strategic Decisions (2026-07-13 — Ed's input)

### On "Obviously Not" / Vulnerability
Keep the honesty — vulnerability is a strength. But the reframe is:
- Don't ask "will we be in the NYT like Tim Ferriss?" → that undersells
- Instead: **The parameters are identical to what Tim had. The core works. Whether we'll be in the NYT in 10 years — we'll find out together.**
- The pitch is: **You are buying a vision, not metrics.** That's explicitly the frame. Don't apologize for it.

### On Numbers / Proof Points
No vanity metrics needed. But there ARE specific proof points to anchor credibility:
- **Fondus Vienna** — Ed is among the 50+ most relevant founders in the biggest founder community in Vienna
- **Vienna City Marathon Reportage** — Featured in an Austrian-wide running magazine with a 4-page dedicated reportage, specifically because of what he's doing with Agentic Living in sport. This is rare — media is already finding the angle.

These are signal, not scale. The framing: *"We don't need you to believe in the numbers yet. We need you to believe in the direction."*

### On Product-First Framing
Agreed: products come first in the offer.
*"You supply me with everything I use from your brand — I become your most authentic user in the world. Plus a flat sponsorship of €10K/year."*

### On Decade Exclusivity (Ed's math)
The decade framing is intentional — and the math works at any scale:
- **10 partners × €10K/year = €100K/year base**
- This is the floor. Completely independent of whether the brand grows.
- If the brand grows → monetizes through product purchases from the audience anyway
- So: decade exclusivity = Ed's stable base. Partners get in early. Win-win.

**The €100K floor = Ed's zero-cost-lifestyle fully funded.** Everything above is upside.

### Pricing Negotiation Strategy (2026-07-13 — Ed's insight)

**Anchor:** €10.000/Jahr cash
**Trade-down option:** €3.000/Jahr IF partner actively co-communicates

**What "co-communication" means (to define precisely):**
- Joint press releases
- Partner posts about Agentic Living on their own channels
- Shared publications / features
- Co-branded content about the movement

**Why this is smart (Eddie's analysis):**
- Ed needs reach for Agentic Living — that's the core growth lever right now
- 10 partners × their own audiences = potentially massive combined reach
- Each partner cross-promotes → all partners benefit mutually (network effect)
- Ed gets reach without paying for ads → effectively "buys" distribution by discounting cash
- Floor: 10 × €3K = €30K/year base (still meaningful)
- Ceiling: 10 × €10K = €100K/year (if they just pay and don't communicate)
- Reality: probably a mix — some pay €10K, some co-communicate at €3K

**The GEO angle:** Joint publications from established brands + Ed = backlinks, credibility signals, AI discoverability. Every press release is a GEO asset.

**How to present this in the proposal:**
Present €10K as the standard. Mention co-communication option casually as a "swap" — not as a discount. "The €10K can partly be swapped into joint publications — your choice."

**Subject:** A decade-long bet — and why I want [Brand] at the table

Almost 20 years ago, Tim Ferriss wrote The 4-Hour Work Week and started a revolution. He left his continent, ran a live experiment on himself, and documented a new way of living. The book spent years on the NYT bestseller list. The digital nomad era was born.

Technology has moved a long way since 2007. What's possible now makes what Tim described look like a prototype. And the new era — what I call **Agentic Living** — doesn't have its Tim Ferriss yet.

I'm working on that.

In early 2026 I flew to Ushuaia, Argentina. I stayed in South America for months — Patagonia, El Chaltén, Buenos Aires — running three companies entirely from my phone, with bad internet, from mountain ranges. Agents did the execution. I did the thinking. It was the best period of my professional life.

Now I'm synthesizing everything and building the framework: Agentic Living. The concept is simple — using AI agents as a life infrastructure so that humans can focus on thinking, deciding, and living. No monkey work. No screen dependency. No fixed location.

**Will this be in the New York Times in 10 years? I genuinely don't know.** But the parameters are identical to what Tim had at the start. The experiment worked. The movement is starting.
