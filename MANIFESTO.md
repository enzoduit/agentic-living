# Agentic Living — Working Manifesto

> Last updated: 2026-07-09 | Status: early draft, actively evolving

---

## The Why

Agentic Living is intentionally not called "Agentic Working". It's not about hustle or productivity optimization. It's about adopting agents as a new way of living — one that focuses on what you want to achieve, and achieves more of that with the help of modern technology.

The core insight: when we find something we're truly passionate about, "work" becomes self-realization — one of the three purposes of life according to Viktor Frankl. The separation between work and life is itself the problem, not the balance.

**The parallel to Tim Ferriss:** The 4-Hour Work Week had one lever — time arbitrage through outsourcing and automation. Agentic Living has a stronger lever: the elimination of execution load, freeing cognitive capacity for thinking, deciding, and experiencing. Not delegated — eliminated. That's a category shift, not an iteration.

**The Patagonia proof:** 49+ days working from phone only, with agents doing all execution, from Patagonia with limited internet connection. The experiment proved that location, connectivity, and device dependency can be removed without losing capability — and in fact, flow states increase.

---

## The Definition

**Agentic Living is to use technology as a means to an end in a way that optimizes human thinking.**

The key implication: we do not use a computer or technology in ways that consume time and energy unnecessarily. Copy-pasting, clicking, formatting, typing — these consume time and attention without adding thinking value. They should not exist.

At its core: **radical ownership over one's own attention.** Mobility is an expression of this, not the essence.

## The 6 Principles

### 1. No Monkey Work
Do not do any manual file or system editing unless it's there to drive focus and support the thinking process. Every action that doesn't require human judgment should be automated.

**Metric:** Time spent on execution tasks with no thinking component (target: near zero)

### 2. Delegation with Ownership
Agents have clear responsibilities and jobs delegated so they can work autonomously — in the same way a trusted employee would. There must be work happening without direct human input. If not, it's just a chat assistant that consumes energy without getting things done.

**Metric:** Results produced without direct human input (% of total outcomes)

### 3. Screen Independence
All actions must be possible without a computer — 100% mobile, low-bandwidth internet. This enables the most productive flow states regardless of location: a coffee in Tilcara, a beach in Altafulla, a bench next to the Río de la Plata.

**Metric:** Times per week you need to go to your laptop to complete a task (target: <2)

### 4. Meetings for Humans Only
Meetings are only for dedicated human-to-human communication: emotional support, connection, and thinking processes. Never for execution, reporting, or status updates that agents could handle.

**Metric:** Unnecessary meetings per month (target: 0)

### 5. Technology for Health
Technology enables personal health, not sacrifices it. This includes physical health (no prolonged sitting) and mental health (complete off-time, not always-on). The goal is more life, not more output.

**Metric:** Personal health/happiness self-assessment (weekly)

### 6. Trust Through Competence
Working with agents is a learnable skill — the Agent Quotient. You can only delegate what you understand well enough to verify. Agentic Living matures as your ability to define, direct, and evaluate agent work matures.

**Metric:** Agent Quotient score (quality of outcomes without direct supervision)

## The FLOW Framework (DEAL equivalent)

*"Get into FLOW."*

**F — Free yourself from monkey work**
Identify every task that requires no thinking and eliminate it from your life. Copy-paste, formatting, clicking, filing — none of it should touch your hands. If a monkey could do it, an agent should.

**L — Load your agents with ownership**
Don't delegate tasks — delegate responsibility. Every agent has KPIs, a mission, and defined autonomy levels. No babysitting. Work happens without your direct input.

**O — Operate from anywhere**
Build every process so it works from your phone, with bad internet, from anywhere on earth. Screen-independence is a design principle, not a bonus feature.

**W — Work at human speed**
Meetings only for humans. Decisions asynchronously where possible. Your time belongs to thinking, connection, and movement — not coordination.

---

### Alternative frameworks considered:
- ARIA (Automate / Reclaim / Install ownership / Anywhere)
- FREE (Free from monkey work / Responsibility to agents / Everywhere-ready / Exit bad meetings)

### Why FLOW wins:
1. It's Ed's own word — used consistently since day 1
2. It describes a state, not a to-do list — mirrors the philosophy
3. "Get into FLOW" works as a CTA across all languages
4. Natural book structure: one chapter per letter

I wanted to run an ultra at the end of the world.

In March 2026 I flew to Ushuaia, Argentina — the southernmost city on earth. The ultra was cancelled after 90km when the first snow of the season hit. But what happened next changed how I work, live, and think.

I stayed in South America. Not because I had to — because I could.

Patagonia. Buenos Aires. El Chaltén, the trail running capital of Argentina, surrounded by glaciers on three sides. From there, I ran three companies. From my phone. With bad internet.

Agents did the execution. I did the thinking.

After 49 days I wrote the longest email I had ever written — to executives in my network. The people with the least time. Because I believed: every person and every company needs this. Better sooner than later.

The response was so positive I knew I needed to share it wider. Not as theory. As a blueprint.

That is Agentic Living.
