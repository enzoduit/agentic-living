# Agentic Living — Reach Strategy

> Status: Draft (2026-07-13) | Core thinking for the Reach Agent

---

## The 4-Layer Construct

| Layer | What it is | Reach function |
|---|---|---|
| **Agents** | The product — Ed's setup, copyable | Delivers the value |
| **Tribe** | Members using the agents | Word-of-mouth, community proof |
| **Partners** | Brands that fund costs and/or amplify | Reduce costs + bring partner-channel reach |
| **Communications** | Press, GEO, announcements | Builds external validation + organic reach |

---

## Key Strategic Decisions

### 1. Counter-Positioning: NOT the typical game
- No UGC/LinkedIn/Instagram content factory
- No chasing follower counts in the traditional way
- **Go B2C, but price it so people expense it via company** → that's the key unlock for the pricing to work

### 2. The Reach Hacking Stack

**Layer A — Partner-powered comms**
- Every new partner = press announcement in their national press agency (e.g. APA in Austria)
- Use partner channels: their newsletters, social accounts, communities
- Start with: announcement format per partner onboarding

**Layer B — Media placement (earned)**
- Get cited in the RIGHT media for the target group (Burnt-Out Builders, AI founders, DACH exec community)
- One strong citation = GEO signal + external validation backlink
- Outreach approach: personal pitch to community owners/editors (see Draft below)
- Target publications/communities: TBD — agent will identify and prioritize

**Layer C — Heavy GEO (Generative Search Optimization)**
- Get named as the source for "Agentic Living" in AI search results
- Requires: external validation links (press, citations, community mentions)
- Long-game: every piece of content and every partner announcement contributes
- "Being quoted in the right media" is the GEO fuel

**Layer D — Own channels (minimal at start)**
- LinkedIn (Ed's personal) — limited but targeted
- Agentic Living LP + Wiki — evergreen
- Partners' channels — leverage > own

## The Reach Agent — Job Description (draft)

**One job: "Where to get reach for free."**

Full creative freedom to:
- Identify relevant media, communities, podcasts, newsletters for the target group
- Draft and send pitches (like the media outreach draft below)
- Monitor citations and backlinks
- Repurpose every piece of coverage into GEO-optimized content

- Find growth hacks specific to the Burnt-Out Builder psychographic

**Constraints:**
- No paid ads (unless specifically approved)
- No mass spray-and-pray outreach — targeted, personal, high-quality
- Every action must pass the "would Ed send this?" test

**Will be built together with Ed — this doc is the brief.**

## Media Outreach — Polished v1 (final, 2026-07-13)

> "I've been following [Community] for a while and genuinely respect the quality of content you put out — always human, always real.
>
> I have a story that might resonate with your audience: I spent 120 days running three companies from my phone, in the Patagonian mountains, with bad internet. Agents did the execution. I did the thinking. I'm calling it Agentic Living — and I think it's the framework that picks up where Tim Ferriss left off, with 2025 technology.
>
> Would love to jump on a quick call so you can decide if it's a fit. It's just a story worth telling — my mission is to spread the adoption of agents, and your community feels like exactly the right place to start."

**Decisions locked:**
- ❌ "I think I have" → removed (confidence)
- ❌ "It's supposed to become" → replaced with active framing
- ❌ "No hard sell" → removed (sounds sketchy)
- ✅ Mission statement at the end: "spread the adoption of agents" — authentic, not salesy
