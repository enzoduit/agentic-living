## Product Used
- **Krisp** — AI meeting transcription & noise cancellation
- Integration: automatic sync to OpenClaw agent within minutes after each call

---

## Why Krisp for Agentic Living

### 1. Background operation — no bot in the call

"It runs in the background so that you don't need to add a bot to your video call or whatever. It always runs in the background and works super smooth."

→ No awkward "bot joined the meeting" moment. No consent friction. It just runs.

### 2. Seamless agent integration

"It's super easy to sync and integrate with any kind of system. I kind of have an automatic sync to my OpenClaw agent — an automated process that runs just a couple of minutes after the call. This makes it super easy to always have the right context set there and use it from my agent without having to think about adding the context extra."

→ **The result:** Every meeting automatically becomes agent context. Within minutes of hanging up, the agent knows what was discussed, what was decided, what needs to happen next. No manual notes. No copy-paste. No "let me brief the agent on this call."

---

## The Broader Point

"It is to integrate your meetings on autopilot — which is a super simple step in most cases if you use the right software. It gives you a lot of ongoing new context, which makes your agent normally always smarter. For me Krisp is a perfect solution."

→ **The first step to going agentic:** Get your meetings into your agent automatically. Krisp solves this.

---

## The In-Person Gap (honest limitation)

"One thing I'm personally struggling a lot with is how to do in-person meetings. I started doing it a little bit with Krisp also because there is a feature for that."

"I was a big fan of the Limitless Pendant. It was then acquired by Meta and shut down. Now I'm experimenting with setting up an open-source system where I can use the hardware — just a small clip that you can put on your clothes — and record everything in person in a very non-intrusive, easy way."

→ **Honest gap:** Video calls = solved with Krisp. In-person = still being figured out. The pendant experiment (open-source hardware + clip microphone) is the current attempt.
