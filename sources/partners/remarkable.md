## Story 1: Always There

"You'll probably never see me without it. It's one thing that I almost all the time have with me because basically all my thoughts are on there and I can read on it."

"It's always actually next to my bed whenever I have some thoughts, so I can put them down — because that's important to get rid of everything in your head to clear up space. This is really what the reMarkable is for."

---

## Story 2: Reading Everywhere — Bike, Trail, Travel

"When I am driving with my bicycle somewhere or running somewhere, to always have a book with me for a good read — which is totally crucial.

I love physical books and I do read physical books whenever I'm at home. But when I'm traveling it's just so much lighter to have just a reMarkable with you. This gives a lot of independence."

→ The reMarkable replaces a library on the road. No weight, no decisions about which books to bring. Everything is there.

---

## Story 3: San Francisco Beta Store

"I saw it in the beta store in San Francisco and then I really wanted to have it — so I have had it for a long time. It's really the first version. It feels a bit like a prototype."

→ **Day-one adopter.** Bought the original reMarkable before most people knew it existed. This is not a recent purchase — it is an 8-year relationship with a product.

---

## Story 4: Paper Pro — Why He Went Back

"I had the Paper Pro but for me it's not durable enough because it has a glass screen because of the color screen and the backlight, which is nice but for me not an absolute must-have. Therefore I'm back on the Remarkable 1."

→ Durability over features. The reMarkable 1 survives everything. The Paper Pro's glass screen doesn't match his use case.

---

## The OKR Connection

"The reMarkable is also the space where I document my personal OKRs — to really have them offline and see whenever I do changes, I strike things through or do checks, etc. For me that's a very important part to have them always with me without needing a screen."

→ **Personal goals, offline, always accessible.** Not in an app. Not in the cloud. On paper that you hold.

---

## The Sketching Use Case

"I personally often need to visualize or sketch out my thoughts — and this is always why I always have it with me."

→ When thinking gets complex, drawing it out is faster than typing. The reMarkable is the thinking surface, not a note-taking app.
