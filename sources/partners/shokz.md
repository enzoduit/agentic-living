## Hack 1: The Earplug Amplifier

"If you want to have more music quality, there's this strange thing with bone conduction: when you close your ear it actually gets louder and more intense. I take advantage of that by having these quiet loops — like mini earplugs, but just mechanical, reducing noise around you. There are many options for that. When you have them in the ear, it actually gets louder and the music quality goes up.

This also has the advantage that you can just put them in if you don't want to listen to anything and get your silence. This is something I truly need in many situations — to have more focus and better thinking when I'm in public spaces."

→ Two modes in one: earplug = silence/focus mode. Earplug + Shokz = enhanced audio mode. No compromise.

---

## Hack 2: The Wind Noise Eliminator (The Microphone Muff)

"The ultimate hack is from old microphones — this overlay that reduces noise, from TV or from old headsets that had it over the microphone. If you buy one of those and put it on one side of the earphones over the microphone, it reduces every kind of wind noise.

I can go 40 km/h with my bike with headwind and it's crystal sharp sound. I can have calls even in the roughest conditions. This is a complete game changer."

→ **40 km/h headwind, crystal sharp audio.** The microphone muff hack = professional-grade call quality while cycling at full speed.
