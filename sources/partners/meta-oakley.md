# Partner Source — Oakley × Meta Smart Glasses (Vanguard)
# Ed's own words only. Raw dictation, 2026-07-21.

---

## Product Journey
- **Generation 1:** Meta Ray-Ban — daily use, nice. But: always had to carry BOTH the Meta glasses AND separate sport glasses for running/cycling. A pain.
- **Generation 2:** Meta Oakley Vanguard — complete game changer. Sport-capable, always on, everywhere.

---

## Why the Vanguard Changed Everything

"When the Meta Oakley came out — the Vanguard — it was like the complete game changer. I'm now having them all the time. Basically I'm running with them, I'm cycling with them. And you can really make absolutely stunning hands-free videos that you could never do before."

→ **One glasses for everything.** Sport + capture in one. No decision, no switching, no compromise.

---

## The Philosophy — Real Point of View

"The coolest thing about it is that you do not interrupt enjoying nature by taking out the phone or something — you're fully in the moment. You can still capture these moments that you really care about.

One thing I personally love is that there's a lot of hype stuff from the point of view (POV) on Instagram, etc. I think the truly only real point of view is if you capture something with your Meta glasses. I truly like that, love that, and live that."

→ **The phone IS the interruption.** The moment you take it out, you leave the experience to document it. The glasses let you stay inside it.

---

## Documenting the Agentic Life

"This is kind of also the way I can document my agentic life. There are also very cool self-talks during my long ultras where I reflect on the situation and share my deepest thoughts — also only possible because I have the glasses with me."

→ The glasses are the capture layer for the whole Agentic Living story. Every photo in this essay. Every moment documented. All hands-free, all in the moment.

---

## The "Necesito un Milagro" Story

"I start a video where you see my point of view and you hear my life reflection — all the way to probably the most complicated moment I had in my running career, in Patagonia, when I was not able to run a single meter more.

I went to an aid station and just told them: 'Necesito un milagro' — which in English is 'I need a miracle' — because I couldn't do another step anymore.

I continued. And after the aid station, I did a video where — in the voice — you hear that I'm absolutely crying. And ten minutes afterwards I was running again."

→ **This is the proof that the glasses are always there.** Not just for beautiful mountain moments — for the hardest moment in the race. The most real moment. Captured because the glasses were on, not because a phone was pulled out.

**Race:** UTMB Ushuaia, March 2026 (the race that was eventually cancelled at 90km due to snow — but this moment happened before the cancellation)
