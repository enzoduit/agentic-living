## The Zurich Story

"I had a business meeting in Zurich and we went with the boat across the lake. I was on the other side of the lake and I thought: this would be a super nice run back — but it was 26 km. As I already expected this to happen I had my notebook and my reMarkable in my running vest and not like a normal backpack.

I also have always water with me because you have these two water flasks that you can put in there — which is so quick and key for hydration. And I just ran back 26 km because I was prepared with the running vest to be super flexible and move fast if I like to in a certain moment."

→ **The proof:** Business meeting in Zurich → spontaneous 26 km run across the lake on the other side. No bags to drop, no excuses, no logistics. The vest was already on.

## The Agentic Living Connection

"That's why I use it a lot — not just for ultra trail running, which it is obviously built for. Also in the city when I'm around and want to move fast and be fully flexible but have a lot of stuff with me. That's why it gives me a lot of freedom."
