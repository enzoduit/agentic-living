## Why Cotopaxi

"I knew that I will be traveling quite a bit. I wanted to have not a full functional backpack and not business stuff — but something that does it all. I could not dream of any better solution than the Cotopaxi."

---

## The Design — Clamshell Opening

"It's the clamshell backpack. You can open it up like a normal suitcase — and this makes it so damn practical. There's not a single thing that I found that doesn't make it practical."

**Layout:**
- **Main half:** one big area — all your stuff
- **Second half:** smaller area — shoes, dirty things
- **Inside pocket:** easily accessible items
- **External pocket:** accessible from outside without opening the clamshell — power bank, food, travel snacks. "The stuff you need during the travel."
- **Laptop/notebook area:** thief-proof. "Super super smart."

→ "Everything about this thing is super smart."

---

## The Huemul Circuit Story (4 days, Southern Patagonian Ice Field)

"I traveled the Circuito Huemul for four days where I put a tent on top — outside, on the very smart leashes that there are. I put my sleeping mattress outside on the straps. You would not expect this from a bag that is, in theory, not a full-functional mountaineering pack.

I had 20 kg and walked for four days all day long — and it was just super comfortable. I am still surprised how this is possible. It felt more practical than the super functional, professional pack that my friend had."

→ **20 kg for 4 days on the Patagonian ice field.** Tent strapped on top, sleeping mattress on the outside straps. This is not what a clamshell travel bag is supposed to do. It did it anyway.

## The Travel Use Case

→ The same bag goes from cabin luggage to 4-day expedition to day bag in the city. No second bag. No decisions.
