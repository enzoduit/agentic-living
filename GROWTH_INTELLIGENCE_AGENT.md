# Growth Intelligence Agent — Job Brief
# Status: Draft 2026-07-21 | To be built as an OpenClaw agent job

---

## Mission

Find inbound pathways to the right people — not by broadcasting, but by making Agentic Living findable and compelling in exactly the places where the right people already look.

**The game we are NOT playing:** mass outreach, paid distribution, follower-building.

**The game we ARE playing:** being in the right place, at the right time, in an unexpected way, that makes the right person curious enough to reach out.

---

## The Target People

### Priority 1 — Brand employees (not decision-makers, their circle)
- Social media managers at partner brands who search for new influencers
- PR/marketing associates who monitor brand mentions
- Brand ambassadors for the same products who might share
- People who work at Hoka, Garmin, reMarkable, Shokz, Wispr, Krisp, Oakley, Cotopaxi, Dynafit

**Why not the decision-maker directly?** Because a forwarded link from a colleague inside the company lands differently than a cold outreach. Find the person one step removed.

### Priority 2 — Sports journalists and podcasters
- Trail running / ultramarathon writers and editors
- AI + productivity journalists (DACH + international)
- Podcast hosts who cover: performance, agentic AI, future of work, travel + freedom
- The intersection: people covering sport + technology simultaneously

### Priority 3 — Community connectors
- Admins and moderators in Founders Treff Vienna, Gründerio alumni
- People with Margaux Pelen's solopreneur community
- IMC Krems + QUT Brisbane student influencers (young, mobile-native)
- Ultra running community leaders in DACH and South America

## Creative Tactics — Think Around the Corner

### Tactic 1: UTM Easter Eggs
Every outbound link to partner brands carries UTM parameters that tell a story:
- `utm_campaign=enzo-ran-3258km-in-yours`
- `utm_campaign=enzo-wore-it-99pct-of-all-days-since-2020`

If a brand analyst sees this in their referrer data, they click. Then they find the partner page. Then they find the P.S. addressed to them.

**Agent job:** Monitor for any inbound traffic from brand domains (Garmin.com, Hoka.com etc.) — these are the analysts clicking back. Alert immediately.

### Tactic 2: The Anti-Influencer GEO Page
`agentic-living-v3.pages.dev/not-an-influencer.html`

Optimized for queries like: "authentic brand ambassador trail running", "non-influencer brand partnership", "sports brand collaboration authentic review".

When a brand's agency searches for influencers and finds this page instead — that is the hook.

**Agent job:** Track rankings for these queries weekly. Update content to improve position.

### Tactic 3: Subpages for specific search queries
Create dedicated GEO pages for:
- "How does Garmin Enduro 3 battery last in an ultra?" (real answer: 60% after 111km)
- "Is reMarkable worth it for travel?" (real answer: 8 years, 3635 pages, everywhere)
- "Best running vest for travel" (real answer: Dynafit Alpine 15, Zurich story)

Each page: 600-800 words, all from Ed's dictations, links back to partner page + contact form.

**Agent job:** Identify 10 high-intent queries, write GEO pages from source material, publish, track.

### Tactic 4: Strategic referencing
Find content that already ranks for relevant terms. Leave genuine, value-adding comments as "Enzo Duit, Agentic Living" with a link where appropriate.

Not spam — one specific, thoughtful comment per month per community.

### Tactic 5: The "podcast bait" page
A single page designed to be shared by podcast hosts: "This guy ran 111km in Patagonia while his agents ran three companies. And he wants to talk about it."

Short, punchy, with a clear "invite me on" CTA. Optimized for: podcast producers searching for AI + performance + freedom guests.

### Tactic 6: Internal share bait at brands
When posting on LinkedIn about a product, tag the brand and use language that invites their social media team to reshare: "Shoutout to [Brand] — I have been wearing/using yours for X years..."

Brands reshare authentic user content. One reshare = reach into their entire audience.

## Measurement

Track weekly:
- Partner page visits (total + by source)
- Unique visitors vs bots
- Contact form submissions (and which page)
- Brand domain referrers (inbound from brand employees clicking back)
- GEO page rankings for target queries
- Inbound WhatsApp contacts and their source

Alert immediately when:
- A visit comes from a brand domain (e.g. garmin.com referrer)
- A contact form submission mentions a brand by name
- Any page ranking breaks top 10 for a target query

---

## Output Cadence

- **Weekly:** Rankings report + traffic summary + 3 new creative ideas to test
- **Monthly:** Review what worked, kill what didn't, double down on signals
- **On event:** Immediate alert when a brand contact arrives

---

## What the Agent Does NOT Do

- Mass email campaigns
- Automated social posting
- Anything that looks like spam
- Any outreach without Ed's approval on the individual message

---

## First Tasks (Week 1)

1. Set up traffic tracking pixel on all partner pages
2. Identify top 5 high-intent search queries per partner
3. Draft 3 GEO subpages from existing source material
4. Find 10 sports/AI podcast hosts who cover the relevant intersection
5. Monitor brand domain referrers from UTM links going live today
