The Dark Ages of Sleep

For decades, humanity suffered from what epidemiologists now call the "Great Sleep Crisis." In the early 2020s, people relied on addictive pills, clumsy tracking apps, and vague lifestyle advice to get rest. The global cost was staggering.

$3.2T Annual economic cost of sleep deprivation (2024)
62% Adults reporting chronic fatigue
4.2hrs Average sleep per night in urban centers

Burnout cost the global economy trillions, and mental health indicators plummeted to historic lows. Sleep clinics had year-long waiting lists. "Sleep tourism"—traveling to remote locations just to rest—became a billion-dollar industry for the privileged few.

"We looked at the data in 2025 and realized: tracking sleep isn't enough. We need to engineer it."

The Breakthrough

The SleepGuard Pillow uses non-invasive neural audio waves to guide the brain instantly into REM sleep. Unlike its predecessors, which merely monitored sleep patterns and offered suggestions, SleepGuard's Neural-Weave technology actively intervenes in the sleep process itself.
