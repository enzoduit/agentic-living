⚠ PHASE 2: RISK ASSESSMENT & ETHICS

The "Black Mirror" Session

The Crisis

📅 THE SCENARIO
2038

It is 3 years after your launch. Your startup was a massive success. But today, an investigative journalist just leaked a scandal that threatens to destroy your company.

THE PROMPT

Every technology has a dark side. You must find yours before the market does.

Your team must strip away the marketing hype and ask:

"Who gets hurt?"

01 Your Task

Identify the one critical failure in your Utopia from the following categories:

🧠

The Addiction

Did you rewire the user's brain so they can't function without you?

⚖️

The Divide

Did you create a massive gap between rich and poor?

🔓

The Data

Are you selling your users' most private thoughts/movements?

⚡

The Bias

Does your AI discriminate against a specific group unintentionally?

02 The Deliverable

CONFIDENTIAL

"The Leaked Memo"

Write a 1-page Internal Confidential Memo
(from the CTO to the CEO)

1

THE CRISIS

Admit what is actually happening (the truth you hid from the public).

2

THE MITIGATION

How do we fix this technically or operationally to save the company?
